HOW IT WORKS

Hold. Talk. Let go.

Pip Dictée types what you say into any app: Mail, Slack, your code editor, a form. Everything happens on this Mac, even with Wi-Fi off.

1
⌃ ⌥

Hold ⌃⌥

Pip wakes up and leans in. A soft tick tells you it's listening. Change the key in Settings.

2

Talk

Say it the way you'd say it. For long thoughts, add Space: hands-free, press ⌃⌥ again to finish.

3

Let go

Pip looks up and thinks for about a second. Whisper runs on this Mac; nothing is uploaded.
